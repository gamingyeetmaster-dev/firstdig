# First Dig trade-proof release gate

Internal proof must use explicitly classified work: teardown for demolition, underpinning for underpinning, and pool for pool work. Do not infer excavation or pool work from a new-house or addition filing. Verify the City permit ID, work description, stage, source refresh time, and successful First Dig ingest before showing any record as current. A permit does not prove a project is seeking bids. Keep commercial email sending disabled until the separate compliance gate is satisfied.
