What First Dig adds to the public record

Source trail first

We organize City and AGCO public records while keeping the underlying filing types visible. A filing is evidence of activity, not a promise that a prospect wants to be contacted.

Stage, not just a row

Project and opening stages can help a vendor decide whether a downstream buying decision may still be ahead. The useful question is what comes next, not simply whether a filing exists.

Joined, not dumped

Opening Soon stacks three independent filings by address. Multiple independent signals at one address can make an opening hypothesis stronger, while the source trail stays visible for verification.
