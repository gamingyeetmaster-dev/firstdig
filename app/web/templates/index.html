How this differs from lead-gen sites

It's the primary record

We read the same permit and licence files the city publishes, every day. No form-fills, no shared leads, no “exclusive” leads sold four times.

Timing from the filing itself

Permit and licence records can surface before the downstream work or opening is obvious publicly. First Dig keeps the filing date and stage visible so you can judge whether the timing is relevant to your sales process.

Joined, not dumped

Opening Soon joins independent filings by address. Two or more signals at one address increase confidence that an opening or fit-out process is underway, but they do not guarantee an opening date.
