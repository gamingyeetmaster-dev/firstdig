Who uses it, and how

Pool and landscape

A teardown filed today has a bare yard in ten months. Send a letter to the owner and the builder while the drawings are still open.

Fence, railing, deck

Filter to "Teardown", "New house" and "Major addition" in your service area. The permit lists the declared value so you can size the job.

Windows, doors, millwork, closets

Builders spec these 3 to 6 months after issuance. The feed shows stage: Applied, Issued, Under construction.

Realtors and lenders

Multiplex and garden-suite filings are future listings and refinances. See them by neighbourhood before the sign goes up.

Trades looking for GCs

The builder name is on the permit. Fifty builders file most of the teardowns in the city. Now you know which ones are busy this month.

Movers and storage

A major addition or underpinning means a family moving out for six months. That is a moving job and a storage unit.
