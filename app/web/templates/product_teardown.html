How to find Toronto building permits and early construction leads

The City of Toronto publishes active building-permit records, but a single construction project can appear as several separate rows for demolition, plumbing, HVAC, drains and revisions. First Dig groups those filings into one project per address so contractors can search the signal instead of manually sorting the raw permit file.

What can a Toronto building permit tell you?

A permit can reveal the address, application timing, project description, stage, declared construction value and—in some filings—the applicant-stated builder. Those fields can help identify a teardown, new custom home, multiplex, garden suite or major addition before the downstream work is awarded.

Who uses construction-permit leads?

Pool builders, landscapers, fence and railing contractors, window and door companies, millwork shops, home-automation firms, movers, trades and other businesses can use permit timing to focus prospecting on properties where substantial work is actually planned.

Who uses it, and how

Pool and landscape

Windows, doors, millwork, closets

Movers and storage
