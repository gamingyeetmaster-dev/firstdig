Terms of service

  • Current access. First Dig is in early access. The product organises public records and adds joining, classification, geocoding and workflow features. Self-serve paid subscriptions are not live on the public site yet.
  • Source licences. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Toronto and the Open Government Licence – Ontario. First Dig is an independent project and does not imply City of Toronto or AGCO endorsement.
  • What you may not do. Resell, redistribute or republish the feeds or exports as a product; use them to send unsolicited electronic messages in breach of CASL; or use them to harass or discriminate against anyone.
  • Accuracy. We reproduce filings as published and add classification and geocoding that can be wrong. No warranty of accuracy, completeness or fitness. Verify before you act.
  • Billing. Public self-serve billing is not active during early access. If paid access is introduced, the applicable price, tax, renewal and cancellation terms will be shown before payment.
  • Changes. Public sources and First Dig features can change. If a source becomes unavailable or a classification changes, we may update or remove affected records or features.
  • Accuracy and reliance. Public filings, classifications and inferred stages can be incomplete or wrong. Verify the underlying source before making a commercial, legal or operational decision.
  • Law. Ontario, Canada.

Privacy

  • What we collect from you. On the public early-access site, you may choose to email us your address, company, role and use case. If account or billing features are enabled later, the product may also store account preferences and payment-provider identifiers needed to operate those features.
  • What we do with it. Respond to early-access requests, operate requested product features, and provide support. We do not sell submitted contact information for advertising.
  • Cookies and analytics. The public site does not use third-party analytics trackers. Account features, when enabled, may use a necessary session cookie to keep you signed in.
  • Personal information in the feeds. Names and phone numbers shown in the feeds are those the applicant filed on a public permit or licence. If you are an applicant and want a record suppressed from our site, email us with the address and we will suppress it within five business days; the public record itself remains with the City or AGCO.
  • Requests and deletion. Email jackson@firstdig.app to ask about information you submitted to First Dig or to request deletion where applicable.
