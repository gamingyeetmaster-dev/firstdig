Three filings, one address

AGCO liquor application

Every new liquor sales licence goes on public notice for 21 days with the premises name and address. This is the earliest, cleanest signal for restaurants and bars.

City business licence

Eating and drinking establishments, take-out, personal services, holistic centres, entertainment venues. Issued with the operating name, the licensee and sometimes a phone number.

Fit-out building permit

Interior alterations for "new restaurant (60 seats)", "dental office fit-out", "tenant fit-up Group E". Comes with the declared construction budget.

Score

Who buys from a business before it opens

  • POS, payments and merchant services — the terminal is chosen in the fit-out month.
  • Commercial insurance brokers — the liquor licence needs proof of insurance to issue.
  • Food, beverage and coffee distributors — accounts are won before the first order, not after.
  • Signage, printing, menus, uniforms, linen, hood cleaning, pest control, waste — every one is a first-90-days purchase.
  • Local media and delivery platforms — onboarding a restaurant a month before opening is worth a year of ads.

We only surface what the businesses themselves filed publicly. We do not scrape social media or private data. Contact details appear only when the applicant put them on a public licence.
