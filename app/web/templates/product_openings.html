Three filings, one address

AGCO liquor application

Liquor-sales licence applications on public notice can provide a premises name and address. First Dig treats that filing as one signal in the source trail, not as proof of an opening date.

City business licence

Municipal business-licence records can add an operating name, licence category and other fields disclosed in the public source.

Fit-out building permit

Commercial interior-alteration and fit-out permits can add project descriptions, stage information and declared construction value from the permit record.

Heuristic score

First Dig weights signal type, additional independent signals, recency and available project value to help sort the feed. It is a prioritization heuristic, not a prediction that a business will open or buy.

Who buys from a business before it opens

  • POS, payments and merchant services — relevant when a new operator is evaluating its payment stack.
  • Commercial insurance brokers — potentially relevant while a new operation is preparing to open.
  • Food, beverage and coffee distributors — potentially relevant when a restaurant or café is preparing suppliers.
  • Signage, printing, menus, uniforms, linen, cleaning and waste — categories to investigate when a location is in fit-out or pre-opening stages.
  • Local media and delivery platforms — may care about identifying new operators before a public launch.

We only surface what the businesses themselves filed publicly. We do not scrape social media or private data. Contact details appear only when the applicant put them on a public licence.
