Questions people ask

Where does the data come from? City of Toronto Open Data and the AGCO public-notice file. First Dig organizes those records and does not append private contact data. Customers remain responsible for using the data and conducting outreach in compliance with applicable law.

How fresh is it? First Dig refreshes its source datasets daily. A record appears after the relevant public source publishes it and the next First Dig refresh completes.

Do you sell leads more than once? This isn't a lead marketplace. It's the public record, organised. Every subscriber sees the same filings; what you do with them first is up to you.

Team access? Request early access and tell us how your team would use the data. We are validating the workflow before opening self-serve team plans.
